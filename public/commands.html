Activities 3 commands

Stock Market 2 commands
/stock
Full stock market access in one command. 15 companies, prices shift every 30 minutes. Buying increases price slightly, selling decreases it.
/stock list | buy [ticker] [shares] | sell [ticker] [shares] | portfolio | history [ticker]
Example: /stock buy NRGX 10
Portfolio shows current value and total gain or loss
History shows last 10 price points with trend indicators
/dividends
Collect passive daily income from your stock holdings. 2% of share price per share per day. Minimum 5 shares per stock to qualify.
/dividends collect | preview | rates
Preview shows pending payout before you commit
Rates shows the full market payout table
24-hour cooldown
Slave System 1 command
/slaveHigh Stakes
Full slave system in one command. Purchase players, manage your empire, and check your own status. The price is 2x the target's wallet balance. They have 2 minutes to outbid — if they don't, they're yours. All their work earnings route to you until their debt is cleared.
/slave buy @user | outbid [amount] | status | panel | list | sell @user
Buy price = target wallet x 2
Debt = 2x buy price - all work earnings reduce it
Panel: free, renew (double debt for half cost), or view stats
Outbid window is 2 minutes from the buy attempt
PvP 3 commands
/robCooldownRisk
Attempt to steal from another player's wallet. Getting caught costs you money.
/rob @user
Target needs at least $50 in wallet
If caught: lose 15% of your balance
/hitman30 min cooldownRisk
Hire an operative to sabotage another player. Choose your tier - higher tiers cost more but succeed more often. On success the operative seizes funds, destroys their daily streak, or wipes their work cooldown. On failure you take blowback damage.
/hitman @user [tier]
Street Thug: $5,000 - 35% success
Enforcer: $25,000 - 55% success
Professional: $100,000 - 72% success
Ghost Operative: $500,000 - 88% success
/will
Designate where your balance goes if you go inactive. Set a beneficiary, amount, inactivity trigger window, and optional final message.
/will set @user [amount] | view | revoke | incoming
Trigger window: 7-90 days of inactivity (default 30)
Amount supports: all, half, 10k, 5m
Incoming: see if anyone has named you as a beneficiary
